FLORIDA MUGSHOTS

Public booking records from Florida county jails, posted to @florida.mugshots.

What this is

Each night we read the previous day's public booking records from Lee, Polk and Orange counties and post a selection. Booking records are public under Florida's public records law (Fla. Stat. ch. 119).

An arrest is not a conviction. Charges are allegations, and everyone is presumed innocent until proven guilty in court. Posts show what was recorded at booking; charges are often reduced or dropped later.

Removal requests

Send @florida.mugshots a DM with the name on the post or a link to it. Removal is always free; we never ask for or accept payment (Fla. Stat. § 901.43). Once removed, that person is left out of all future posts.
