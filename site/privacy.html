Privacy Policy

Florida Mugshots · Last updated October 10, 2026

What we publish

Names, ages, booking photos, charges, bond amounts, arresting agencies and booking dates from public county booking records. We do not publish addresses, dates of birth or anyone under 18.

Instagram data

Our app uses the Instagram API only for our own account (@florida.mugshots): to publish posts, read and reply to direct messages sent to us, moderate comments on our posts, and read our own post insights. We do not collect data about other Instagram users, sell data, or use it for advertising. Messages are used only to handle the request they contain, such as a removal.

Removal and deletion

To have a post removed or your data deleted, DM @florida.mugshots. Removal is free, and removed people are excluded from all future posts. We keep only a one-way hash of the name so we can honor the request.

Terms

Content is provided as-is from public records and may be out of date. An arrest is not a conviction; everyone is presumed innocent until proven guilty in court. Do not use this content to harass anyone.
